# Emberline — Kestrel Yard: three.js tactical FPS

A round-based, Counter-Strike-style tactical FPS for the make.fun
`threejs-fps-02` bounty ($1,000, 10 places, 1st $400). Built and tested
15–16 Nov 2026. **Not submitted** — the parent agent handles the repo,
the transcript bundle, and the submission.

## What it is

Vanguard (attackers, teal) vs Sentinel (defenders, amber), 4v4, first to
5 rounds (max 9). One map, Kestrel Yard (64×48 m, dusk): Foundry site A
west, Silo site B east, three lanes (top / mid through a container maze /
bottom). The spike (bomb) is carried by a Vanguard, planted at a site
(hold E, 3.5 s), 35 s fuse, defused in 6 s. A round ends by elimination,
detonation, defuse, or timeout (defenders win if nothing is planted).

- **Weapons:** P9 pistol (free), MP9 SMG $900, VK-47 rifle $1,700,
  Longshot sniper $2,600 (right-click 4× zoom), kevlar $650, frag $300,
  smoke $250. Economy: start $800, win +$325, loss +$190, kill +$300,
  plant +$300. Buy phase 12 s before each round; buy menu with B or keys
  1–6 (keys buy while the menu is open, otherwise they swap weapons).
- **Gunplay:** hitscan with headshot spheres (2×) and body boxes, damage
  falloff, recoil + spread that grows while moving and firing, reloads,
  running out of ammo, tracers, muzzle flash, camera shake, hitmarker,
  damage-direction indicator, low-health vignette.
- **Bots:** 7 bots on a nav graph with A* routes. They hold positions,
  rotate to the spike, escort the carrier, plant/defuse, buy their own
  loadouts, react to sight (vision cone + line of sight + smoke) and
  sound (shots within 26 m), strafe, burst fire with aim error that
  shrinks as they track you, crouch on long angles, and throw grenades.
  The spike carrier keeps pushing the site instead of stopping to fight.
- **Player:** WASD, mouse look (pointer lock, drag fallback), click fire,
  R reload, C/Ctrl crouch, Shift walk, Space jump, G grenade, 1/2/3
  weapons, E plant/defuse (hold), B buy menu, Tab scoreboard, M mute.
- **Presentation:** dusk sky shader, warm sun + hemisphere light, shadows,
  procedural ground texture (lane dashes, site rings A/B, hazard stripes),
  container yard with amber edge stripes, silo cylinders, distant city
  silhouettes, fighter models with name tags, viewmodel gun with bob and
  recoil kick, pooled tracers/particles/smoke, minimap (enemies only when
  spotted), kill feed, phase banners, menu/buy/dead/match-end overlays.
  All sounds are synthesized WebAudio — no audio files, no network.
- **Tech:** fixed-step simulation at 60 Hz, apart from rendering; one
  state object; the renderer and HUD only read it. Seeded RNG
  (mulberry32), so `?seed=N` replays identically.

## How to run and test

```sh
cd ~/workspace/makefun-fps
node serve.ts --port 8091        # serves game/ on http://localhost:8091/
# open http://localhost:8091/ in desktop Chrome, click to play
```

Staged scenes for screenshots (deterministic, `?seed=1`):

- `/?shot=start&seed=1` — menu over the yard
- `/?shot=combat&seed=1` — mid-round, rifle firing, enemies ahead
- `/?shot=buy&seed=1` — buy phase, $2,400, menu open
- `/?shot=end&seed=1` — round won, spike detonated at site A

Hand-in program:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

Kit check (from inside the unpacked kit):

```sh
node cli/makefun.mjs check --kit . --repo ~/workspace/makefun-fps
```

## Bugs found and fixed during testing

- `siloMat is not defined` (render.js) — a material name that never
  existed; the silo now uses an inline steel material.
- The viewmodel gun's bright teal accent strip read as a giant floating
  plank — scaled the viewmodel to 0.62 and dimmed the strip.
- The player's own body mesh and "You" name tag were visible mid-map:
  the render loop skips syncing the player's mesh, so it sat at the
  world origin. The mesh is now hidden at creation.
- Buy keys conflicted with weapon slots (1–3 swapped weapons even with
  the buy menu open). Number keys now buy while the menu is open.
- **Determinism bug:** the bot scan sweep reads `f.id`, and the id came
  from a module-level counter that was never reset — two runs of the
  same seed diverged. `createSim` now resets the id counter.
- **Site A was unreachable:** nav node 4 sat inside the Foundry wall,
  so it had 0 edges and no bot could ever path to site A. The node
  moved to (-22,-8), open ground inside the site circle; the top- and
  bottom-lane midpoints (nodes 8 and 11) were also isolated and were
  moved to (12,-18) and (14,18) — all nodes now have ≥1 edge.
- **Bots never planted:** `resetFighter` reset the bot's path but not
  its `dest`, so a bot whose carried-over destination matched its new
  objective never re-pathed and stood still. `dest` is now reset too.
  The carrier also used to stop and strafe-fight instead of pushing;
  it now keeps advancing to the site while still shooting.
- **Bots could not defuse:** a defuser stopped wherever it entered the
  site (up to 5 m from the spike) but the defuse only counts within
  1.6 m. A defusing bot now walks to the spike itself before standing
  still.

## Honest limits

- **No local grade exists.** The judge is server-side (AI, pairwise,
  superbot, no ties). The starter handed in unchanged plays as house
  game #2; this entry is original code and art, but its pairwise score
  against the house games and the other ~5,700 entries is unknown.
- **The game was never played in a real desktop Chrome** — only in
  headless Chromium under SwiftShader. Rendering is slow there (the sim
  runs at ~15–30% of wall speed), which is a headless-software-rendering
  artifact, not a game bug: the loop caps at 5 fixed steps per frame,
  so on a slow renderer sim time lags wall time. On real hardware at
  60 fps it runs in real time. Pointer lock, audio, and the feel of
  mouse look were not verified by hand.
- **Bot balance is rough.** In bot-only matches the Sentinels win most
  rounds and rounds often end by elimination in under a minute; the
  plant happens (3/3 matches) but detonation/defuse decides few rounds.
  Friendly fire from crossfire happens (bots shoot through teammates
  who walk into their line of fire) — realistic, but frequent.
- **The top lane is thinly connected** (node 8 has 6 edges after the
  fix, but crossing the lane still routes around a container); the map
  plays mostly through mid and bottom.
- **No Docker grade-local run** — the kit's grade-local for a judge
  competition needs the server-side judge, so there is nothing to run
  locally beyond `check`.
- The `?shot=` staging is deterministic for the same seed, but the
  combat shot's exact tracer placement varies with render timing
  (screenshots were re-taken after the final code changes).
